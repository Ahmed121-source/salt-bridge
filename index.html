About us

Egyptian Salt. Global Supply.

Salt Bridge Global supplies Egyptian salt products to distributors and commercial buyers across the United States, Canada and Europe. From premium Siwa Oasis rock salt for deicing and industrial applications to refined and specialty salts, we offer grades, grain sizes and packaging tailored to customer specifications.

Our Egyptian operations bring more than a decade of experience in salt production and worldwide exports. With our new office in New York, Salt Bridge Global is expanding its presence to provide closer customer support and coordinate supply for the U.S. market.

We serve wholesale distributors, snow-removal and landscaping contractors, retailers, schools, shopping centers, property managers and suppliers fulfilling government road-salt contracts.

Our product range includes washed and crushed rock salt, screened salt, fine and coarse industrial salt, water-softener tablets, high-purity vacuum salt, refined table salt and sea salt. Supply options include bulk vessel shipments, containerized cargo, jumbo bags and retail bags.

Our operations team coordinates inland transportation, warehousing, bagging, port handling and shipping, including bulk-vessel chartering arrangements. Whether you need bagged products for distribution or bulk salt for seasonal demand, we work with you to match the product, packaging and shipment to your requirements.

The source

Naturally white salt from Egypt’s Western Desert

Our rock salt comes from salt production sites in Egypt’s Siwa Oasis, where it is harvested from naturally saline lakes and prepared for processing and shipment.

  • 98.5% or more sodium chloride, with moisture of 1% or less
  • Naturally white colour
  • Screened to the grain size your application needs
