The source

Naturally white salt from Egypt’s Western Desert

Our rock salt comes from the quarries of the Siwa Oasis, where salt is lifted from brilliant turquoise salt lakes and stockpiled under the desert sun. It is a raw, natural salt that is high in sodium chloride and low in moisture.

  • 98% or more sodium chloride, with moisture of 1% or less
  • Naturally white colour
  • Screened to the grain size your application needs
